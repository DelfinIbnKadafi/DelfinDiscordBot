Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use Delfin Discord Bot, how that information is handled, and which external services are involved in providing its features.

1. Information We Process

The Bot processes information required to receive and respond to your Discord interactions. This may include command inputs, message content submitted through supported features, and information made available through Discord's interaction system.

Discord may independently collect and process account, usage, and interaction information according to its own privacy policy.

2. How Information Is Used

Information received by the Bot is used to process commands, provide requested features, and deliver responses through Discord.

When you use the /ask command, your submitted prompt is forwarded to an external provider to generate a response, which is then returned to you through Discord.

The Bot's current implementation does not store prompts or generated responses in a persistent application database. Technical information related to errors may appear in the hosting environment's logs for troubleshooting purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Delfin Discord Bot relies on external services to operate and provide certain features. These services may process information as part of their normal operation.

The primary services currently involved are:

  • Discord: Provides the platform through which users interact with the Bot.
  • Groq: Processes prompts submitted through the /ask command and provides the resulting responses.

Each provider operates under its own terms, privacy policy, and data handling practices. Their data retention, security measures, and processing activities are not directly controlled by the Bot.

You can learn more through their respective policies: Discord Privacy Policy and Groq Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Security

The Bot does not maintain a persistent application database for storing command prompts or responses. However, some technical information may be temporarily or permanently retained in server logs, depending on the hosting environment and its configuration.

External service providers may also retain information processed through their platforms according to their respective policies.

Reasonable measures are taken to keep the Bot and its operating environment secure. However, no online service or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your Privacy and Choices

You are free to decide whether or not to use the Bot and its available features. You should avoid submitting passwords, financial information, personal records, or other sensitive or confidential information through its commands.

For information or privacy requests concerning data handled by Discord or Groq, please contact the respective provider directly, as such data is subject to their own privacy practices.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's features, infrastructure, or data handling practices change.

Any revisions will be published on this page, with the effective date updated accordingly. We encourage users to review this page occasionally to stay informed about how information is handled.

7. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how the Bot handles information, you can contact the project maintainer through the project's GitHub repository.

For information about the rules and conditions that apply when using the Bot, please read our Terms of Service.
