Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Delfin Discord Bot (the “Bot”) handles information when you interact with it on Discord. It describes the Bot’s current behavior and does not replace the privacy policies of Discord or other service providers.

1. Information processed

The Bot responds to Discord slash commands. Discord may also process account and interaction information as described in its own privacy policy.

2. How information is used

The ai prompt is used to request an AI-generated answer and return that answer to you on Discord. The Bot’s current code does not save prompts or responses to an application database. Technical errors may be written to the Bot host’s logs for troubleshooting.

3. Third-party services

Discord provides the platform on which the Bot operates, and Groq processes prompts submitted through /ask. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy practices. Their processing, retention, and safeguards are outside the Bot’s control. Review the Discord Privacy Policy and Groq Privacy Policy for further information.

4. Retention and security

The Bot does not maintain a persistent application store for prompts or responses. Limited technical logs may be retained by the environment that hosts the Bot, and third-party providers may retain information under their own policies. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices

You can choose not to use the Bot or not to submit a prompt. Do not include passwords, financial details, health information, or other sensitive or confidential information in a prompt. Requests concerning information held by Discord or Groq should be directed to those providers.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the Bot or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or the Bot’s data handling, contact the project maintainer through the project’s GitHub repository.

Please also review the Terms of Service for the conditions that apply to use of the Bot.
